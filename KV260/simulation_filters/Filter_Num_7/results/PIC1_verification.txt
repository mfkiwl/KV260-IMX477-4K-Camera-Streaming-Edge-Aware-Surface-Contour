A remaining difference at the hairline
Coordinates use a top-left origin. At (1431,139) and (1443,153) the finishing stage received uniform 9 × 9 neighborhoods of (1, 25, 32). The final capture therefore contains (1, 25, 32) at both locations. The reference requires (28, 57, 63) and (1, 26, 33), respectively. Changing the final smoothing weights alone cannot distinguish these two inputs.

Calibration scope
