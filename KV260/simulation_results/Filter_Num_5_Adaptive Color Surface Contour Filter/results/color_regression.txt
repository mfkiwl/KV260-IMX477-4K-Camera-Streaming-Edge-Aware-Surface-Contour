Filter 5 color regression
=========================

NVC 1.23.1, VHDL-2008, tb_pic2, aligned Designer1 path, G_COLOR_PROFILE=auto.
Default tap spacings 1/3/6 and contour spacing 6. JPEG decoding ran in VHDL.
G_STALLS=true inserted source gaps and randomized sink backpressure.

luma subject to the common 8-bit RGB gamut limit. The three accepted BMPs matched in full,
including their headers. Shared luma shifts can still alter RGB brightness and normalized saturation.

---------------------------------------------------

span reaches at least 32. For colored source pixels (span at least 24), count a lost or shifted hue
when output span falls below 12 or circular HSV hue differs by more than 30 degrees.
All quantities use 8-bit RGB. Both columns in each comparison use the same source mask.

Additional RTL checks
---------------------
GHDL 5.0.1 passed both directions of a back-to-back 43 x 27 profile switch without reset
under stalls. The captures matched the forced-profile references. All 1,161 grayscale ramp
pixels had R=G=B. The dedicated four-entry FIFO test passed with 288 accepted pixels,
address wrap, stalls, luma targets at gamut limits and aligned SOF/EOL markers.

GHDL synthesis elaborated the default 2048 x 1152 design and inferred an 84,136 x 18 RAM
for source chroma. GHDL ignores the vendor RAM style attribute. Device mapping, resource
utilization and post-route timing require a Vivado implementation.
